About

Bullpen Phone is a free, non-commercial, automated tracker of MLB trades, free agents and front office moves. It has no reporters and breaks no news. All credit belongs to the journalists and outlets it links to.

How it works

About every 10 minutes, software reads the sources below, recognizes players and clubs in each headline, and groups reports into threads. Timing depends on GitHub's scheduler, so stories usually appear 15 to 20 minutes after a source publishes them, and sometimes later. This isn't the fastest place for breaking news.

How stories are ordered

The board puts concrete news first. Reports from reporters, stories with several sources, and stronger statuses rank higher, and every story fades with age. It's a simple formula rather than an editor's judgment.

What a status means

  • Linked: a report connects the player with a club, often speculatively.
  • Interest: a report says a club is interested or pursuing.
  • Talks: a report describes negotiations.
  • Agreement reported: a report describes a done deal. It may not be final.
  • Fell through: a report says talks ended.
  • Official: MLB's own transaction data shows the move. Only this data can set it.
  • Unclassified: the software wasn't confident enough to label it, or the label hasn't passed its accuracy check.

Known limits

  • It reads headlines, so it can misread them. Check the original report.
  • “First seen here” is when this site found a report, not who broke the story.
  • It doesn't read X, where much baseball news breaks first, so stories that appear only there can be missed. Small staff moves are often missed too.
  • Fan sites are labeled as such, are limited to concrete news, and never change a player's status.
  • If a reporter deletes a Bluesky post, it leaves this site on the next update.
  • Most Bluesky accounts carry a verification badge. A few don't, and are included because several verified reporters follow them.
  • Players who share a name are only grouped when a report names a known club.
  • It has no contract history, payroll data, projections or original analysis.
